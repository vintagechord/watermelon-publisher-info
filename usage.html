Operator usage terms

Effective October 16, 2026. WaterMelon YouTube Automation is a tool for the WaterMelon channel owner and operators personally authorized by that owner. It is not a public account or upload service.

YouTube and Google

By using this app, operators agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The app uses YouTube API Services and must be used consistently with their applicable policies. Read the app's privacy policy before authorizing Google access.

Content and publishing control

  • Use only preview audio, artwork and other material that the channel owner is authorized to publish for promotion. This app does not grant music or artwork rights.
  • The owner chooses the channel, publishing scope and final video visibility. Uploads start privately so processing, thumbnails and source availability can be checked.
  • The owner may review and edit generated titles, descriptions and tags before first upload. Seller credit and the relevant product link should remain accurate.
  • The app can pause future work, resume it, or disconnect Google access. Confirmed source withdrawal can make its corresponding video private. These checks are periodic.
  • Use YouTube Studio to review claims, restrictions, audience declarations and already-published videos. Local data deletion or disconnection does not delete published videos.

Independent operation

The worker runs on the owner's separate computer. It must not be installed on WaterMelon's serving host or given the service's database credentials. Its source reads, local cache and daily upload attempts are limited. The operator must keep the computer running for automatic work to continue and protect the single persistent publishing database against loss or simultaneous copies.

These instructions do not change WaterMelon prices, music licenses, purchase rules or seller agreements. This documentation does not create a hosting subscription or any new payment obligation.
